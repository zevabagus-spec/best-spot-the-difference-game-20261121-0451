Lanternlight Twins — a spot-the-difference hunt in three lantern-lit scenes. Two near-identical pictures side by side, ten differences hiding in each: a harbour at dusk, a moonlit garden, a starlight market. Click a difference in either picture to ring it in gold, chain finds into combos, spend hints when you are stuck, and beat the 3-minute lantern before it burns down. All art is hand-drawn in canvas, all sound is synthesised in WebAudio, all names are original. No build step, no network: open it and hunt.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click a difference in Picture A or Picture B to mark it. H spends a hint, P pauses, R restarts the scene, N moves to the next scene, M mutes, 1–3 pick a scene from the menu.

Staged screenshot scenes (seeded, deterministic) live behind `?shot=cover|garden|market|win`, as listed in `shots.json`.
